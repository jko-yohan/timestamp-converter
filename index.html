What is a Unix Timestamp?

A Unix timestamp (also known as Epoch time or POSIX time) is the number of seconds that have elapsed since January 1, 1970, 00:00:00 UTC. This converter helps you translate between Unix timestamps and human-readable dates in both directions.

Features

  • Shows UTC and local time
